Privacy policy

Your phone is your remote. Here’s what Beam uses to connect, cast and manage Pro access.

Effective 28 October 2026

Who provides Beam

Beam is provided by the developer identified on its App Store listing, operating as wirebuilds. Contact wirebuilds@icloud.com for privacy questions.

TV discovery and pairing

Beam uses your local network to discover compatible TVs and send remote commands. This involves local IP addresses, device names, model information and supported controls. Recent-TV history and app preferences are stored on your phone. Pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Beam does not send TV pairing credentials to RevenueCat or operate a server that receives your TV commands.

Photos and videos you choose

Beam accesses media that you select for casting. For Google Cast or a compatible TV receiver, your phone makes the selected file available to that receiver over your local network while sharing. AirPlay uses Apple’s playback and routing services. Temporary media copies or converted files may be created on your phone to support playback, and are released when sharing is stopped or replaced. Beam does not upload your photo library to a wirebuilds server.

Voice and text

Dictation uses your microphone and Apple’s Speech framework. Depending on your device, language and Apple’s settings, Apple may process speech on-device or through its services. Beam does not keep an audio-recording archive. The transcript appears in the editor for you to review; text is sent to the connected TV when you choose Send.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments. Beam uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access and manage Pro entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase history and a randomly generated customer identifier, together with technical information necessary to operate the purchase service. Purchase information is also used for subscription analytics. Beam does not require a Beam account, and does not give RevenueCat your TV credentials, media library or voice recordings. Apple payment-card details are not provided to Beam.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Google Cast

Google’s Cast SDK processes device identifiers, product-interaction information and other diagnostic information for app functionality and analytics. This may include technical information about casting sessions and receiver devices. See Google’s privacy policy. Cast SDK data handling is separate from the local TV history that Beam stores on your phone.

App diagnostics and Google Analytics

Beam uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand feature usage and investigate failed connections, playback errors and crashes. These services process an app-installation identifier, app and device technical details, usage events, crash reports and diagnostic information. Google Analytics also derives approximate location, such as country or city, from network information; Beam does not request precise location access. These records can be associated with the same app installation, but Beam does not require an account or send your name or email in its custom analytics events. Our custom events contain fixed categories, error codes and timings; they exclude TV addresses and names, selected media, voice recordings, transcripts and purchase receipts. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled.

You can turn off collection in Beam Settings → Diagnostics. A bounded recent-activity report is also kept on your phone and can be shared with support when you choose. Firebase’s processing and retention are governed by Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy. This website itself does not run Google Analytics.

Support and this website

If you email support, we use your address, message and information you choose to include to respond and resolve the issue. This website contains no advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices and retention

You can manage Local Network, Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions in iPhone Settings, stop sharing from the Cast screen, and forget recent TVs in Beam. Deleting the app removes its app-container data, but iOS Keychain items may persist. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records according to their policies and applicable requirements; deleting Beam does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we can identify as yours. We may need information to locate the relevant record, and may retain records needed for legal obligations or unresolved transactions. Your rights depend on where you live. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Tracking, children and updates

Beam does not use your data for targeted advertising or track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It is a general-purpose utility, not a service directed at children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
