BEAM SUPPORT

Let’s get connected.

A few useful checks, then a real person if you need help.

Connect your TV

  1. Turn on the TV and leave it awake.
  2. Connect your iPhone and TV to the same home Wi-Fi. Guest networks may isolate devices.
  3. Allow Beam to use Local Network in iPhone Settings. Enable mobile control on the TV if it provides that setting.
  4. If you use a VPN, allow local-device connections or pause it while pairing.
  5. Choose the TV in Beam. Approve any request on the TV, or enter the code it shows.

Use the step-by-step connection guide in Beam if your TV does not appear. Some TVs cannot be discovered while powered off.

Why is a button unavailable?

Beam adapts controls to the connected device. Streaming players and simulators can expose fewer controls than a television. A Pro purchase does not add controls that the TV itself cannot support.

Type or use voice

Open a search or text field on the TV first. In Beam, open the keyboard composer, type or use its microphone, review the text, then send it. Text support depends on the TV and the TV app. Dictation also needs microphone and speech permissions.

Cast photos and videos

Choose a supported Google Cast, TV receiver or AirPlay route in the Cast tab. Select media and use the visible stop control to end sharing. Receiver support varies by model and media format. Keep Beam open while a TV reads media from the phone over your local network.

Restore or cancel Pro

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy Pro. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings; uninstalling Beam does not cancel billing. Request Apple refunds at Report a Problem.

Contact support

Email wirebuilds@icloud.com. Include your TV model and operating system, iPhone/iOS version, Beam version and a short description of what happens. Do not send passwords, payment details or pairing codes.
